## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the acceptable use of JOL information, systems, and assets, and the security
awareness obligations of personnel. It implements ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls **A.5.10**,
**A.5.11**, and **A.6.3**, and SOC 2 criteria **CC1.1** and **CC2.2**.

## 2. Scope

Applies to everyone who accesses JOL systems, networks, data, or equipment — employees,
contractors, and authorised third parties — regardless of location or device.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| JOL assets | Systems, networks, data, devices, accounts, and services owned or operated by JOL |
| Acceptable use | Use that is lawful, authorised, and consistent with this policy |
| Personal data | Data as defined by GDPR Art. 4 |

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

- **All personnel** — use JOL assets only as authorised; protect credentials; report misuse.
- **Managers** — ensure their teams understand and follow this policy.
- **CISO / HR** — own this policy, deliver awareness training, and administer sanctions.

## 5. Principles

Use of JOL assets is a privilege conditioned on lawful, ethical, and secure behaviour. Personnel
have no expectation of privacy over JOL systems, which may be monitored to the extent permitted by
EU and member-state law.

## 6. Policy Statements

### 6.1 Acceptable Use of Assets and Systems

1. JOL assets **must** be used only for legitimate JOL business and in accordance with this policy
   suite and applicable law.
2. Personnel **must not** use JOL assets for unlawful activity, to access unauthorised data, to
   circumvent security controls, or to introduce malware.
3. Credentials and authentication factors **must** remain confidential, **must not** be shared, and
   **must** be used only by their assigned holder (see
   [POL-ACC-003](access-control-policy.md)).
4. Personal data **must** be handled in accordance with
   [POL-CLS-006](data-classification-and-handling.md) and GDPR; it **must not** be copied to
   unauthorised devices, personal accounts, or non-EU locations without a lawful transfer mechanism.
5. Monitoring, logging, and auditing of JOL systems **may** be performed for security and
   compliance, to the extent permitted by law.

### 6.2 Return of Assets on Termination

1. On termination or role change, all JOL assets (devices, media, tokens, documents) and access
   **must** be returned or revoked promptly, and in any case before the individual's last working
   day ends (see [POL-ACC-003 §6.5](access-control-policy.md)).
2. HR and the CISO **must** coordinate a de-provisioning checklist as part of leaver processing.

### 6.3 Security Awareness and Training

1. All personnel **must** complete security and data-protection awareness training at onboarding
   and at least annually.
2. Personnel in privileged or high-risk roles **must** complete additional, role-specific training.
3. Completion **must** be recorded and reviewable as control evidence (see `jol-compliance-evidence`).

### 6.4 Sanctions for Misuse

1. Suspected misuse **must** be reported to the CISO.
2. Confirmed violations **must** result in proportionate action, up to and including termination and
   legal proceedings, applied consistently and documented.

## 7. Enforcement and Sanctions

Enforcement follows §6.4 and [POL-ISM-001 §7](information-security-policy.md).

## 8. Exceptions

Exceptions **must** be time-bound, risk-assessed, and approved via the
[Policy Exception Request](../templates/policy-exception-request.md) form.

## 9. Review

The CISO reviews this policy at least annually and following any significant incident or legal change.
